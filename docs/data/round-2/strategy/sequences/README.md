## The sequence (every contact gets all four)

| Touch | Day | Subject | Angle | Signature |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | 0 | segment-specific (e.g. `after the round`) | Their world + the raise + their **cost of being wrong**. P1 = personalized opener. | Credibility-tagged (Microsoft/FP Complete) |
| **2** | 3 | `re: <t1 subject>` (threaded) | Aaron's pedigree + Levana ($1B+). Offers **examples**, not a meeting. | Minimal |
| **3** | 7 | `one thought` | The reframe — earns the reply by being true, no hard CTA. | Minimal |
| **4** | 14 | `closing the loop` | Break-up bump. Easy out. (Often the highest single-touch reply.) | Minimal |

**The CTA is never the calendar — cold.** Touches ask for *interest* ("worth a look?"). The moment anyone
replies positively: **stop the sequence** and send the calendar ask (`reply_calendar_ask` column / [doc 04](../04-sequences-and-copy.md#after-they-reply--then-ask-for-the-calendar)) — a specific Tue/Thu time. **Reply handling is the #1 meeting lever: a human in the loop within hours.**

## Rollout to maximize meetings

1. **Wave 1 — `ready` (319).** Send in **cohorts of ≤50** (≈2.8× better reply than big blasts). Lead with
   **P1 (120)** — they have the personalized opener, the highest-converting touch #1. Then **P2 (192)**.
   Filter: `send_readiness == ready`, order by `tier` (P1 first), then send ≤50/cohort.
2. **Wave 2 — `verify_email` (143).** Verify the P3 emails first, then send the same 4 touches.
3. **Wave 3 — `verify_domain` (52)** after a 30-second domain confirm; `optional` (22) if you want reach.
4. **Parallel — `needs_name` (4) + the 262 no-email companies** → LinkedIn/X scripts (doc 04).
5. Track **reply rate by segment/tier**, not opens. Double down on the best "cost of being wrong" angle; A/B
   backlog in [doc 04](../04-sequences-and-copy.md#quick-ab-backlog-once-you-have-volume).

Benchmark from the strategy: segmented + triggered + founder-signed → ~5–12% replies, ~1–4% meetings →
realistically **~10–15 meetings from email alone** before multichannel/warm-intro lift.

## Must-fix before the first send (the bodies are done; the plumbing isn't)

- [ ] **Footer placeholders** — every body contains `[STREET ADDRESS], [CITY, COUNTRY]` and `[UNSUBSCRIBE LINK]`.
      Fill both (valid postal address + one-click opt-out) — they're a CAN-SPAM / CNIL requirement. Find-and-replace
      across the CSVs, or set the real values in the script's `FOOTER` constant and re-run.
- [ ] **SendGrid for cold** — dedicated subuser + IP, SPF/DKIM(2048)/DMARC green, throttle 20–50/mailbox/day,
      **open-tracking pixel OFF on touch #1** ([03 §1](../03-cold-email-playbook.md)).
- [ ] **Reply-handling owner assigned** — replies pause the sequence and get a human response within hours.
- [ ] **P1 personalized openers eyeballed** — highest-leverage, highest-risk line; skim `t1_body` for the 120 P1.
