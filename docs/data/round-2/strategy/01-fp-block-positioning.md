## The one-sentence positioning

**FP Block builds and rescues software systems where being wrong is more expensive than slowing down.**

Not "a blockchain dev shop." Not "an audit firm." A senior engineering partner for teams running systems
where a single mistake is *irreversible* — money moves and can't be clawed back, a failure is public and
can't be quietly reset, a design choice gets locked in and can't be refactored away.

That framing is deliberate and it is the whole game: **if the problem sounds real without the word
"blockchain," it is real.** Our prospects happen to be in crypto, but we never sell "crypto engineering."
We sell *correctness under permanence.*

## The ICP test (who we're actually after)

A team is a real ICP when **4 of 5** are true:

1. Being wrong carries a clear **financial, reputational, or legal** cost.
2. That cost is **persistent or externally visible**.
3. They are **already aware** of the risk.
4. Decisions **accelerate** once clarity appears.
5. They value **avoiding irreversible regret** over maximizing short-term speed.

**Cost-of-being-wrong (at least one must be present):** real money/assets at risk · public failure / trust
erosion · compliance scrutiny or liability.

**Disqualifiers (any one dominant → pause):** MVP-to-get-funded · "we'll fix it later" · no individual
downside owner · internal-only consequences · needs to be *convinced* that correctness matters · tool-first
buyers · pays for delivery but not thinking.

> A company that just raised a real round to run a system where **other people's money moves** passes the
> test almost by definition. That is exactly who is on the list.

## Language rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE — this is where most outreach dies)

**Lead with:** permanence · ownership · irreversibility · incentives · trust boundaries · the cost of being
wrong · what breaks *after* launch.

**Avoid leading with:** blockchain · DeFi · Web3 · on-chain · crypto · smart contracts · TVL · rollup · ZK ·
"protocol engineering" · "full-stack blockchain development."

**Why this matters for THIS list:** the source CSV ships an `FP Block Outreach Angle` column that does the
*exact opposite* — it opens with "smart contract development," "blockchain protocol engineering,"
"DeFi solutions." **Do not use that column.** It is generic, it sounds like every other agency in their inbox,
and it violates FP Block's own positioning. We rewrite every angle around stakes.

| ❌ Off-brand (from the CSV) | ✅ On-brand (what we send) |
|---|---|
| "FP Block can provide smart contract development for your DeFi platform" | "The hard part of a perps exchange isn't launch — it's the first liquidation cascade you can't undo." |
| "Our blockchain protocol engineering and security audits…" | "When real money moves through your system, 'close enough' becomes a refund you can't issue." |
| "We help scale your Web3 infrastructure" | "Other teams now build on top of you. Your bugs just became their outages." |

**Tone:** confident, plain, a little understated. We are the senior engineer in the room, not a vendor.
We never hype. We name the uncomfortable truth and let it sit.
